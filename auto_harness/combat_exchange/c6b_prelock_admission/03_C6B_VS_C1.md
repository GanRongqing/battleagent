# C6B vs C1 — mechanism difference

- C1 = **post-threat reactive retreat**: triggered AFTER being locked / no kill opportunity / far support
  → moves away. Historical DEV reversal → DROPPED.
- C6B = **pre-lock admission control**: never enters the bad geometry in the first place — a forward
  combat USV that is overextended from its local friendly support while still WITHOUT own lock is held
  back (lateral shaping) until support closes or it can commit safely.
Difference: C6B gates COMMITMENT (before lock); C1 reacts to THREAT (after lock). C6B has no
retreat-to-home, no post-lock retreat, no truth.
