# Negative / Protected Cases
SOFT_REASSIGNMENT_REJECTIONS.csv lists rejected proposals with reasons:
NEAR_LOCK_PROTECTED (~1205 of naive B moves), UNIQUE_INTERCEPTOR_PROTECTED (26),
COVERAGE_UNSAFE (26), MARGINAL_GAIN_ONLY/AMBIGUOUS (weak/ambiguous evidence sets). These
prove the C/D gates exist to prevent bad reassignment, not to inflate change counts.
